{scene}
Style: an unposed documentary photograph taken on a 35mm camera. Natural available light, true-to-life colours, visible film grain, slightly imperfect framing, real textures and everyday clutter. Looks like a photo a person took, not a render: no glossy surfaces, no neon glow, no perfect symmetry.
Setting: a small startup office, a coworking space, a university library, a cafe or a city street in India when it fits the scene.
Composition: portrait 4:5. Keep the lower third calm and simple, because a headline will sit there.
No text, letters, screens with readable words, logos or watermarks. People only as anonymous figures, faces turned away or out of focus.
